## Decision

One paragraph, unambiguous, in the present tense.

## Why

The asymmetry that decided it. Usually: which way of being wrong is cheaper to recover from?

## Consequences

What becomes true, and what obligations this creates for future work.

## Operational impact
What on-call now has to know, run, and rehearse. Include what this *removes*, not only what it adds.

## Cost impact
Direction, magnitude, and what drives it. Distinguish fixed from marginal.

## Security impact
Both directions. A decision with only positive security impact has not been examined.

## Scalability impact
Where it stops working, and what the next move is when it does.

## Reversal criteria *(recommended for high-risk decisions)*
The measurable conditions under which we would revisit this. Pre-committing these is what turns a bet into a plan.
